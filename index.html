Decision Points

Two questions, every time.

Before a case closes, agents work through evidence and return requirements in that order.

DECISION POINT 1

Is evidence required?

Low-value orderAgent discretion
Clear fulfillment errorMay waive evidence
High-value orderEvidence required
Repeat claimantEvidence required
DECISION POINT 2

Is return required?

Low-value itemUsually no
High-value itemUsually yes
Customized itemManager review
Hygiene-sensitive itemUsually no
Required Information from Customer

Collect this before resolving the case.

Mandatory

  • Order number
  • Customer email address
  • Description of issue
For low-value items, agents may skip photo requirements at their discretion if the claim appears credible.

Evidence requested — customer should provide

  1. Photo of the item receivedConfirms condition and contents against the order.
  2. Photo of packagingIf available — helps identify shipping or handling damage.
  3. Photo of SKU / barcode labelIf applicable — verifies the correct item was sent.
  4. Photo of packing slipIf included — cross-references order details.
Exceptions

Situations that call for case-by-case review.

01

Limited Evidence

When customers cannot provide photos due to disposal or accessibility issues.

  • One-time courtesy replacement
  • Partial refund
  • Manager review
02

Seasonal Peak Periods

During holiday seasons, reporting windows may be extended.

03

VIP or High‑Lifetime‑Value Customers

Management may approve exceptions to preserve customer relationships.

04

International Shipments

Additional transit-related damage reviews may be required depending on carrier claims processes.

Escalation Rules

Escalate to a Team Lead or Manager when…

Financial Risk

  • Refund exceeds predefined threshold (e.g., $500)
  • Multiple cancellation requests from same customer
  • Suspected fraud

Technical Issues

  • Store errors
  • Payment gateway discrepancies
  • Refund processing failures

Operational Issues

  • Warehouse dispute
  • Shipment interception required
  • Inventory discrepancies

Customer Risk

  • VIP customer
  • Public complaint on social media
  • Threatened chargeback
  • Threatened legal action
