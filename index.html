About RugaOps
Noémi, Founder

My background in customer support gave me firsthand experience with the everyday problems that can make support complicated — repetitive requests, unclear processes, inconsistent handling, and situations where the right process isn't always obvious.

I created RugaOps to focus on the part of customer support that often gets overlooked: the system behind the conversations.

My goal is to help small businesses create clearer processes, better documentation, practical workflows, and support systems that make everyday work easier.
